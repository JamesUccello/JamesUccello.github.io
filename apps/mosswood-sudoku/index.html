Mosswood Sudoku

A calm Sudoku that grows a garden. 1,500 offline puzzles, from quick 6×6 to Expert.

FAQ

Does it need internet?
No. All 1,500 puzzles, your progress and your garden live on your phone. Ads only load when you happen to be online.

How do hints work?
You get 3 free hints per puzzle. After that you can watch a short video for another. If you're offline, the hint is free.

How does the garden grow?
Every solved puzzle counts. New plants unlock at 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 16, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 75 puzzles. Four rare seasonal plants grow only when you solve a Daily Puzzle in winter, spring, summer or autumn. Tap any spot in the terrarium to choose which plants are on display.

I'm new to Sudoku. Where do I start?
Try Seedling 6×6. It uses numbers 1–6 on a smaller board, so puzzles take a couple of minutes. You can replay the rules anytime under Settings → How to play.

What are Field Notes?
Your stats page, with best and average times for every level, your Daily streak, and 12 badges to earn.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it.
