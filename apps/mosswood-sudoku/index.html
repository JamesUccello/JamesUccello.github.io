Cozy number puzzles that grow a garden. Works fully offline.

FAQ

Does it need internet?
No. All 1,200 puzzles, your progress and your garden live on your phone. Ads only load when you happen to be online.

How do hints work?
You get 3 free hints per puzzle. After that you can watch a short video for another. If you're offline, the hint is free.

How does the garden grow?
Every solved puzzle counts. New plants unlock at 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 16, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 75 puzzles.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it.
