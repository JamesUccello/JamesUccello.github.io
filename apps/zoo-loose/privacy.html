Privacy Policy – Zoo Loose

Last updated November 23, 2026

Zoo Loose ("the app") is made by James Uccello. This policy explains what information the app handles.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and it never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your stages, stars, tokens, outfits and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. When your device is online, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when asked
  • Approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address
  • Advertising data and product interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs and launch time

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: you can allow or deny tracking when the app asks, and change it later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent: where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Offline: when your device is offline, no ad requests are made.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to general-audience (G-rated) content.
