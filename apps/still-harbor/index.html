Still Harbor: Breathe & Focus

A calm harbor for breathing, live soundscapes and focus timers. Free, no account, no ads during sessions.

Support

Questions or ideas? Email cpdigicomm@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How it works

Breathe: pick a rhythm and a length. The water rises as you breathe in and falls as you breathe out; a soft tap marks each turn.

Sounds: tap layers to mix rain, waves, wind and more. Every sound is generated live by the app. Save mixes and use the sleep timer to fade out.

Focus: choose a watch (15, 25 or 50 minutes) and the number of rounds. Rests come in between.

Harbor Log: every calm minute adds to your harbor scene. Listening to sounds counts at half speed.

FAQ

Do sounds keep playing when I lock my phone?
Yes, sounds are designed to keep playing with the screen locked. Breathing sessions need the screen on to show the guide.

Why do sounds play with the silent switch on?
Like other sound apps, Still Harbor plays through the silent switch so your soundscape isn't cut off. Use the volume buttons or the Sound switch in Settings.

Does it need internet?
No. Everything works offline. Ads only load when you happen to be online, and never during a session.

Is this medical advice?
No. Still Harbor is for relaxation and focus and is not a medical device. If you feel unwell during breathing, stop and breathe normally.
